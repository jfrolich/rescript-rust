- `rescript verify` tries to prove each `@law` for every value of its parameters, from the law's code: by computing with unknowns, by cases, by structural induction on lists and recursive variants, and with the laws proved earlier in the file. A proved law is reported as `proved for every value`; any other is run on values as before, with the reason no proof was found. `@law(proved)` marks a law that must be proved. See `docs/verify/proof.md`.
